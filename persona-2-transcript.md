# Persona 2 Transcript — Arjun, 31, Product Manager

**Testing Date:** April 18, 2026  
**Persona:** Arjun is a 31-year-old PM at a mid-size startup. He had a productive day on paper — shipped a feature, cleared his inbox — but feels strangely empty. He can't explain why.

---

**Tree:** How are you feeling as the day ends?

**Arjun:** Mixed — it had its moments.

**Tree:** What felt heavier today — your tasks or your emotions?

**Arjun:** My thoughts and emotions were heavy.

**Tree:** Did you talk to someone about how you felt, or did you keep it to yourself?

**Arjun:** *[long pause]* I kept it inside. I don't really... talk about this stuff.

**Tree:** It's okay, but don't make it a habit.
> *"Sometimes we need quiet to process. But keeping everything inside too long makes it heavier."*
>
> 💡 Tonight: Write 3 lines in a journal or notes app. What happened. How it felt. What you wish was different.

---

## Evaluator Notes

**Did the question make him pause?** Yes — "Did you talk to someone or keep it to yourself?" hit differently for Arjun. He admitted something he doesn't usually admit.

**Was the path logical?** Yes. The tree correctly detected that his heaviness was emotional (Axis 3 — concern for self) rather than task-related.

**What worked well:** The question "what felt heavier — tasks or emotions?" is a strong differentiator. It separates surface-level productivity stress from deeper emotional states. Most reflection tools don't make this distinction.

**What could be improved:** After "I kept it inside," the tree could ask "Is there someone you trust that you could reach out to tonight?" to nudge toward Axis 3 (widening concern to include others).

**Overall:** Arjun ended the session saying the tree "asked something I didn't expect." That's the goal.
